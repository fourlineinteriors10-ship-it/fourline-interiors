The Studio

Architectural Rigor. Artisanal Finish.

Fourline Interiors was founded with a singular conviction: that exceptional interior design is only as meaningful as the craft and discipline behind its physical execution.

Our Ethos

Bridging Imagination and Construction.

Founded in Mumbai, Fourline Interiors has grown into a renowned interior architecture and turnkey fit-out practice. We operate an integrated model where architects, interior designers, 3D visualization artists, structural engineers, and master woodworkers collaborate seamlessly under one roof.

This end-to-end framework eliminates the typical frictions, delays, and budget escalations of traditional interior contracting. When you engage Fourline, you work with a singular, accountable partner from initial spatial concept through the celebratory handover of your completed home.

"True luxury resides in the invisible joints, the tactile warmth of stone, and the effortless tranquility of a well-proportioned room."

Principal Architect
